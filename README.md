# What's the Answer to 00950215?                                               
                                                                             
The question of 00950215 has haunted librarians, locksmiths, and at least one
  very confused dentist for the better part of a century. The leading zeros
are not decorative — they are load-bearing. Remove them and the entire       
structure collapses into the merely six-digit, which any toddler can
pronounce. Keep them, and you are dealing with a bureaucratic number, and
bureaucratic numbers, as we know, always resolve to the same answer: a filing
  cabinet.

Specifically, 00950215 refers to the third drawer from the bottom of the     
beige metal filing cabinet located in the northwest corner of every municipal
  office ever constructed. You have seen this cabinet. You have walked past   
it. You have, on at least one occasion, been told that the form you need is
"in there somewhere," and then watched a person in a cardigan open the
drawer, stare into it for ninety seconds, and close it again without comment.

Inside drawer 00950215 is a single manila folder. Inside the folder is a     
photocopy of another folder. Inside that folder is a Post-it note that reads,
  in faded pencil, "see 00950216." Drawer 00950216 does not exist. It has     
never existed. A memo was issued in 1973 explaining why, but the memo is
filed in 00950215, which brings us neatly back to where we started.

This is the answer to 00950215. It is also, coincidentally, the answer to    
most questions you will ever ask a government.
